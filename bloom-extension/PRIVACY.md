# Bloom for Desktop: Privacy

- No network requests. No analytics. No remote code. No external fonts or libraries.
- Runs only on instagram.com. Permissions: `storage` and access to instagram.com.
- Stores on your device only: on/off, Strict mode, Hide Reels links, and a daily count of Reels stopped.
- Never reads, saves or sends your messages, account details or anything you type.
- Source code is public: https://github.com/jakadwangdu/Bloom
